HOLLOW DOME

A quiet game about six parrots, a very large empty house, and the man who still has to feed them.

Keyboard required. Headphones recommended. Everything you see is drawn, not photographed.

Contains psychological horror themes, depictions of depression and suicidal ideation (non-instructional), blood and surreal imagery. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone. You are not the only one keeping something alive.
